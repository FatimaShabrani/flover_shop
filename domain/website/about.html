WHO WE ARE

Flowers made for meaningful moments.

Flover is a Jordanian flower and gift shop based in Aqaba. We bring together fresh natural flowers, elegant bouquets, thoughtful gift packages, and floral arrangements for different occasions.

Whether you are celebrating, saying thank you, or letting someone know you care, we help you choose something meaningful — beautifully arranged and thoughtfully presented.

OUR STORY

A simple idea, made with care.

Flover began with a simple idea: to make flowers and thoughtful gifts easier to choose and order.

We create beautiful arrangements that help people express love, appreciation, celebration, and care — turning a thoughtful gesture into a special moment.
